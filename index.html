Rob Jones
Knowledge, evidence and learning — and the AI that helps make sense of it

Hi, I'm Rob. I'm passionate about knowledge, evidence and learning, and about using AI to help people find and use what an organisation already knows.

I'm currently a Research Knowledge Manager at Monzo, in User Research, where I design research taxonomies and build AI-powered synthesis workflows. Before that I spent five years at Save the Children International, latterly as Knowledge Management and Learning Systems Advisor, where I led the build of a global knowledge system for 25,000 staff and piloted an enterprise generative AI search tool. Earlier, I worked with Practical Action as a Knowledge and Learning Officer.

I'm a graduate of the Centre for Development and Emergency Practice (CENDEP) at Oxford Brookes University, with an MA in Development and Emergency Practice. Several of my roles have been based abroad, including in Nepal and Madagascar.
